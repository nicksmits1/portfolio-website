Layout options

Two decisions, each with three options, all built in draft E's palette, type and landscape. Click any landscape to run the solver, and use the skill chips in the second set; they work.

Pick one per decision, or mix: "B, with skills as S1" is a fine answer. Section content comes from your CV and project files; the wording is placeholder. Dashed boxes are assets you would upload (headshot) or links still to come (ORCID).

What came from johnspeaks.com

Taken

  • Headshot, a short bio and every link on the first screen.
  • Experience and Education as sections of their own (merged into one timeline here; they can split).
  • A CV link in the nav. A public CV needs a copy without your phone number.
  • Dated recent posts, as "Latest" in option B.
  • Teasers on home that open full pages: each project row opens /projects/<id>.

Left out, tell me if you want them

  • Leadership. Your CV lists none. Teaching, TA work, reviewing or student groups would go here.
  • A separate Contact page. The peach footer does that job on every page.
  • Writing or notes. Still undecided in the product brief.
  • Google Scholar. Added beside ORCID once you have a profile.
