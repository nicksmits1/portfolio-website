Section headers for Publications and Experience

Both pages get a 320 px header in the place the project pages carry their landscape strip: a character grid drawn live in the site's ramp, with the page title sitting on a calm patch of it. Each header animates and settles to a still under reduced motion.

Publications gets three takes on optimisation in ASCII, after ascii-magic's density, braille and box renderings. Experience gets three takes on the path through the roles, from Lhoist to the PhD. Pick one of each, or mix.

Publications

A page of its own at /publications, newest first, ready to grow through the PhD. The two entries below each header are the real ones going in now.

P1

Character landscape

Himmelblau's function shaded in a ten-step character ramp, each band in its own colour, drifting slowly. Gradient descent runs from a fresh start every few seconds and leaves its iterates in coral, ending on a minimum. The closest relative of the home hero, in another medium.

Phone, 390 px

  • Reads asThe site's objective landscape again, made of type. Strongest continuity with home.
  • MotionSlow rotation of the surface, one descent every six seconds, readout of iterations and f(x) at the bottom right.
  • Reduced motionOne converged descent drawn on a still surface.
P2

Braille surface

A two-minimum surface in 3D, drawn as a wire mesh in braille dots (eight per character, so the lines stay fine), turning slowly. A coral iterate rolls downhill on it. Closest to the hero's dive, where the map turns into a mesh.

Phone, 390 px

  • Reads asA surface you could hold. Finer and more technical than P1; the mesh sits to the right of the title.
  • MotionA full turn every 40 seconds; the iterate restarts from the rim when it settles.
  • Reduced motionThe mesh at a three-quarter view with the iterate's path down to the minimum.
P3

Scenario tree

Stochastic programming drawn in box characters: a first-stage decision x, branching on uncertainty ξ into second- and third-stage recourse. Sampled scenarios run root to leaf in coral and pile up into a cost distribution at the right edge. The PhD's own subject rather than the site's metaphor.

Phone, 390 px

  • Reads asDecisions under uncertainty. Most specific to the PhD topics; least like the rest of the site.
  • MotionA scenario every half second; the histogram fills, then resets.
  • Reduced motionThe tree with a filled distribution and one highlighted path.

Experience

The existing page, with the plain title block replaced by a header. All three are drawn from the real roles and dates; the PhD runs January 2027 to an end between September and December 2030.

E1

Flowsheet

The career as a process flow diagram in box characters: the Lhoist reactor, the hydrate crystalliser at McGill, the BEng separator, the CMU column and a PhD product drum still filling. Stream characters run along the pipes. Chemical engineering's own drawing convention.

Phone, 390 px

  • Reads asPlayful and unmistakably ChemE. Each unit is a role, each label a year.
  • MotionFlow along the pipes; the PhD drum's level rises slowly.
  • Reduced motionThe flowsheet at rest with streams marked and the drum part full.
E2

Strata

Each role a sediment layer in shade characters, oldest at the bottom, each with its own texture and band colour and a year at its edge. The PhD layer is still being laid down: characters fall and settle on top.

Phone, 390 px

  • Reads asDepth and accumulation; matches the descent theme without repeating the landscape.
  • MotionFalling deposit on the top layer only; everything below is still.
  • Reduced motionThe layers as they stand, the top one part laid.
E3

LED timeline

A dot-matrix display, one dot per month from 2022 to 2030, one row per track. Lit runs are the roles; a coral column marks now; the PhD's run beyond it pulses, and its last months fade because the end date is still open. The most informative of the three.

Phone, 390 px

  • Reads asAn actual chart of the CV at a glance. On phones a dot is a quarter, not a month.
  • MotionA scan sweeps to now and lights the runs; the future pulses.
  • Reduced motionAll runs lit, now marked.
