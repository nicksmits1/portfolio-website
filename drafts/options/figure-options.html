Amortised cost

Greedy search pays its whole cost again for every network: each added arc means simulating every candidate over every failure scenario, about six CPU-hours for one 70-node network. The policy pays once, in training, about six to seven hours per seed on a shared 32-core server. After that, a new network costs one forward pass per added arc, about one CPU-second, including networks four times larger than any it trained on.

Proschi-style system diagrams

Proschi's visual grammar in the site palette: cards outlined by kind with a hard offset shadow, an icon tile, square ports, orthogonal edges with labels in pills, and dashed groups. Kinds take colours from the contour ramp. Services are indigo, stores violet, streams coral, third-party services peach and people lavender-grey. The travelling request stays coral.

Kind labels Monospace adds JetBrains Mono as a fourth face and stays closest to Proschi. Onest uppercase keeps the site at three faces.
