Landscape

Four functions instead of four deep bowls, each with one minimum per topic. They are shown with 1A's calm floor under the name so the shape of the terrain is what differs; pair any of them with any treatment above. Three are standard test functions from optimisation, which is the subject of the PhD.

2A

Himmelblau

f(x, y) = (x² + y − 11)² + (x + y² − 7)² has exactly four minima, all at f = 0, joined by saddles around one central peak. Four topics, four equal minima, and a name nobody needs to have heard of to see it is a real function.

  • Reads asA four-leaf basin with ridges between the topics; the upper left rises to coral and peach above the name.
  • SolverA start on a ridge can stall on a saddle between two topics, which the readout already reports.
  • CostThe count is fixed at four; a fifth topic would need its own small bowl added to the function.
2B

Rosenbrock valley

f(x, y) = (1 − x)² + 100(y − x²)², drawn on a log scale: one long curved valley sweeps across the map, and the topics are pools along its floor. Gradient descent is famously slow in this valley, and you can watch it zigzag.

  • Reads asA river through high ground: one dark ribbon from top left to top right, the name below it on the calm floor.
  • SolverFalls straight into the valley, then crawls along it to the nearest pool; long runs, many iterations.
  • CostMost of the map is high ground in coral and peach, a warmer, brighter hero than today's.
2C

Tilted plane

The whole surface slopes from peach top right down to ink at the lower left, with the topics as shallow dimples on the slope. Bands run diagonally like a horizon, and the darkest ground is under the name by construction.

  • Reads asThe calmest of the four: long parallel bands, four small pockets, very few closed rings.
  • SolverAnything that misses a dimple rolls all the way down to Contact, a neat line for the readout.
  • CostThe topic minima are small, so the map says "four places" less loudly than bowls do.
2D

Ripple field

A Rastrigin-style egg crate of small local minima over a gentle slope, with the four topics as the deep basins among them. The ripples calm to nothing under the name.

  • Reads asTextured ground, closer to a contour map of real terrain; the busiest option.
  • SolverMost clicks get stuck in a small local minimum, and the readout says so; only starts near a topic reach it.
  • CostThe texture competes with the labels, and a visitor may need two or three clicks to land on a topic.

Choosing

Round two needs two picks: 1A or 1B, and one of 3A, 3B or 3C. Open after that: whether the chosen function holds for the rest of the visit, and whether project strips follow it.

Whatever is chosen is built into landscape.ts and home.ts; project pages share the function, so a new landscape also changes the 300 px strip on every project page.
